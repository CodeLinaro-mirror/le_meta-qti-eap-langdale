========== Building telematics image for iMX6q sabrelite =====================

bitbake telematics-image

==============================================================================

Other common targets to build
-----------------------------
    core-image-minimal : Minimal image with only a shell and basic apps
    -c do_populate_sdk : to generate the SDK image for the platform


Other aliases
-------------
    rebake <recipe> : to cleanall of recipe's components and rebuild.
    buildclean : to remove the entire platform build and re-build all.
